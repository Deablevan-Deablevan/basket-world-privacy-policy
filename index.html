Privacy Policy

Basket World - Arveksi Games
Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Arveksi Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you play Basket World ("the Game").

By using Basket World, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Game and which features you access, we and our service providers may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: An optional username and identifiers associated with your PlayFab account. Creating an account is not required to access all gameplay features. We do not require an email address to create a username-based game account.
  • Gameplay Information: Level progress, completed levels, scores, achievements, in-game currency such as gold, gameplay activity, and other game statistics.
  • Device and Technical Information: Device identifiers, installation identifiers, device type, operating system, app version, and technical diagnostics.
  • Network Information: IP address and information that may be used to estimate your approximate geographical location.
  • Advertising Information: Advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and information used to deliver and measure advertisements.
  • Analytics Information: Gameplay events, session information, feature usage, progression, and interactions within the Game.
  • Purchase Information: Information about in-app purchases, such as product identifiers, purchase status, and transaction-related records.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information for purposes including:

  • Providing and maintaining the Game.
  • Saving and synchronizing player progress.
  • Managing optional player accounts and usernames.
  • Managing in-game currency, purchases, and game statistics.
  • Understanding gameplay behavior and improving game features.
  • Measuring advertising performance and campaign effectiveness.
  • Displaying advertisements where permitted by applicable law.
  • Detecting fraud, abuse, technical problems, and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the Game.

4. Third-Party Services

Basket World uses third-party services that may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. These services may process information on our behalf or for their own purposes, depending on their roles and settings.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the Game. AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and advertising interactions to deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements, subject to applicable laws and user preferences.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Firebase and Google Analytics

We use Firebase and Google Analytics to understand how players use the Game, measure gameplay events, analyze level progression, evaluate engagement, and improve the player experience. These services may process device identifiers, app activity, technical information, and usage data.

Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Unity Analytics

We use Unity Analytics to collect and analyze gameplay and technical information, including game events, player activity, device identifiers, and IP addresses, to understand game performance and improve the Game.

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Microsoft PlayFab

We use Microsoft PlayFab for optional player accounts, game data management, player statistics, usernames, and synchronization of gameplay information such as level progression and in-game currency.

PlayFab may process account identifiers, device information, IP addresses, login information, and approximate location derived from IP addresses.

Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

5. Advertising and Personalized Ads

Basket World may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your location, age, consent choices, and applicable advertising regulations, advertisements may be personalized using advertising identifiers and other permitted information.

Personalized advertising may not be available or permitted for every user or in every region. Where required, we will respect applicable consent requirements and advertising restrictions.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and privacy controls here: Google's Advertising Policies .

6. In-App Purchases

Basket World offers optional in-app purchases through the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

Payments are processed by the relevant platform provider. We do not directly collect or store your full credit card or payment account details. We may receive purchase-related information, such as product identifiers and transaction confirmation details, to validate purchases and provide purchased content.

Please refer to the privacy policies of the relevant store:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

7. Internet Connection and Permissions

Basket World requires an internet connection for its online services and certain game features.

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or photo gallery.

Network access is used to connect to game services, analytics providers, advertising services, and app store purchase systems.

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone customer database.

Information may be shared with the third-party service providers described in this policy to operate the Game, provide analytics, deliver advertisements, process purchases, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable contractual arrangements.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain player accounts, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
